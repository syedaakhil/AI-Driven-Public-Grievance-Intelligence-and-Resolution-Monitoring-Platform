Frequently asked questions

Who can sign in to this dashboard?

Corporation admins sign in with a username and password. Citizens sign in with their mobile number and an OTP, and use the complaint registration page.

Where is complaint data stored?

In this portal's own database (SQLite). Officers, notices and complaints can also be managed at /admin/.

Privacy note — what happens to the data entered?

Complaints, enquiries and photos stay in this portal's database and media folder. Nothing is sent to a government system or to analytics services.

OTP codes are sent only through the SMS provider configured in .env. Fonts, charts and the optional street map load from public CDNs and never receive form data.

Where do the dashboard numbers come from?

They are counted from the complaints in the database. With no complaints yet, the dashboard shows zeros.

The charts or street map are not showing.

They need an internet connection to load their libraries. Everything else works offline, and the illustrated ward map is always available.
