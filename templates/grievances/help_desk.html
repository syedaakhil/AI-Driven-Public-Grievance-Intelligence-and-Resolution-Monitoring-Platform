How a complaint is handled

  1. RegisteredThe complaint is recorded with its category, ward and the channel it came through.
  2. AssignedIt goes to the officer responsible for that service in that ward.
  3. In progressThe field team works on it; every step is noted on the complaint.
  4. ResolvedThe department closes it, and the citizen can be told using the complaint ID.

Frequently asked questions

There is no water supply in a street. What should we do?

Check City news & notices on the Home page for planned maintenance first. Otherwise file it under Water supply; the target time is 48 hours.

Where do potholes and broken streetlights go?

Both go under Roads & potholes. Mention the nearest landmark so the field team can find the spot quickly.

Sewage is overflowing on a road. Is this high priority?

Yes. Sewage overflow and open manholes are health and safety risks. The complaint form suggests High priority when the description mentions them.

Garbage has not been collected for days.

File it under Sanitation & waste; missed collection has a target time of 24 hours.

A complaint came in on WhatsApp or social media. How is it recorded?

File it as usual and set Received through to the channel it came from. The Home dashboard then shows how many complaints arrive through each channel.

How can a citizen know the complaint is being handled?

Share the complaint ID. Its status moves from Submitted → Assigned → In progress → Resolved, and can be checked with Track a complaint.
